## 1 · Coleco Telstar: Level 1

**Version 1**

1. Hi, I'm Sparky! You know these machines from the outside. Follow me in, and I'll show you what's still glowing.
2. Wakey wakey, Granny! Few guess from the outside, but your name hides your past: COnnecticut LEather COmpany.
3. Go right using the directional input!

**Version 2**

1. Hi, I'm Sparky! Wakey wakey, Granny! Your name hides your past: COnnecticut LEather COmpany.  ✏️
2. Move with WASD or the arrow keys. I zip along a copper trace to the next stop.  ✏️
3. Follow the traces to the Goal and this old console lights up again!  ✏️
